# Product context

register: product

## Product purpose

Recent Cycle is a local-first Chrome extension that gives browser tabs the speed and muscle memory of desktop app switching. A fast tap switches immediately. Holding the modifier reveals a compact preview list for deliberate selection.

## Users

People who keep many browser tabs open and want a keyboard-first way to move through their most recently used tabs without accounts, telemetry, or visual noise.

## Tone and principles

- Calm, precise, and keyboard-native.
- Consistent task surfaces are more valuable than decorative novelty.
- Show state clearly: ready, restricted, connected, warning, selected, and empty.
- Keep the browser-only path useful; the macOS companion is optional coverage.
- Prefer reusable primitives over page-specific visual inventions.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon terminal cosplay, excessive glow, nested cards, oversized marketing layouts, and dense micro-copy that makes a small popup harder to scan.
